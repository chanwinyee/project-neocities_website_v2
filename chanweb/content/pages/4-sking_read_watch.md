## <u>Stephen King Read-Watch</u>
I started on this project as a way to get warmed up to watching horror movies. I find reading horror much easier to do than watching it. 

The rules are simple. Read the story then watch the earliest movie going in writing order. 

This restricts the books/stories to include <i>only</i> the ones that became a movie. 

My process is to reference [www.stephenking.com](https://stephenking.com/works/novel/) once I am ready for the next story. I then use IMDB to find the earliest movie made of that story.
