Summary: 

I asked several of my friends whether the personal blog is dead. Because the tone of a lot of blogs is commercial, I feel that the authenticity that I used to bring to my blogs of high school would stick out like a sore thumb. I'm not withholding because of that. I guess, I am withholding because I am afraid to put myself out there on the internet should it come back to find me.

----

This weekend, M was out of town to take some dirt-biking classes in the middle of nowhere. So, I invited A over for a sleepover and girls night with J. The weekend started off amazingly with a successful girl's night complete with l'apero and a humble but easy and delicious braised pork belly dish. I had planned to make two entrees, but after setting up the charkuterie plate I realized that a second entree would be too much. After dinner, I made hong kong egg waffles and had a silly time with my close circle lounging about.

Honestly, my heart is so full. I have really close friends who have stood the test of time, in the good times and the bad, and the sad days of our lowest moments. 

The next morning, A and I went on a run to my favourite area of Berkeley for breakfast and a nice meander. For some reason, the donut shop was giving free donuts, so our morning - while tough - was rewarded indulgently. We walked for quite some time afterwards to find a suitable breakfast spot where we hungout and chatted as if we were at a bistro in Europe. It was so relaxing with fresh hot coffee refills keeping us warm in the fall morning. 

A part of me wishes I could fully describe how that wandering day felt. While my body was tired and my face a bit sunburnt, something - not quite my mind and not quite my body - felt light and chipper.
