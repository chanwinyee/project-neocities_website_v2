Mental Wellness and "Soma"

Posted on: Mon 09 December 2024

Today, I took a mental wellness day. For some reason, the idea of showing up for work felt repulsive. If there is ever a good time to listen to your heart, it is when there is this acute of a feeling.

I spent the day catching up on the pile of work that accrued for my personal life. It's hard to imagine that at some point in my life, I had ample time to do my day job and have room in my mind to imagine up other projects - and actually do them.

I sometimes believe that the American culture of constant hustling sets us back from higher levels of thinking because of the decision fatigue, brain-drain, soul-sucking that people often attribute to "job." Higher levels of thinking can include being a good friend or partner, taking up a hobby, exploring other aspects of life to discover what is actually meaningful to you, etc.

Have you ever read "A Brave New World" by Alduous Huxley? I don't remember much, but what I do remember is the soma. Soma was a substance that felt good, was taken by everyone (normalized), and muted the thoughts that took people from neutral and complacent to inspired and curious.

In writing this, I wonder if, as adults, we are so filled with obligation that we no longer have capacity for the hard work of curiosity. There are so many blogs that talk about "boredom breeds creativity" and that we are so preoccupied by our phones/jobs/the-algorithm that we are not who we want to be.

... I'm not sure where I am going with this. Just expressing a current state of mind, I suppose.

Anyways, I made an email sorting program that will help me reduce my gmail space after years of archiving versus deleting garbage emails. I wanted to write about that, but got distracted because I have a bit of guilt about taking a mental health day when I know I shouldn't feel guilty about that.
