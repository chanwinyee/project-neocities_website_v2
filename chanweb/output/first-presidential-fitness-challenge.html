The FIRST Presidential Fitness Challenge

Posted on: Mon 15 September 2025

It was Sunday on the hottest day of a recent heatwave. Twelve of my unsuspecting closest friends arrived at a local park expecting "Field Games and BBQ." Meanwhile, I was expecting a revolt as soon as I revelaed my loosely veiled aspiration to relive on of the more humiliating experiences of our mandatory Phys.Ed classes in school - the Presidential Fitness Challenge. The goal wasn't to relive the humiliation, but rather to have a structured out-of-doors event that I could repeat year after year.

To the one unitiated adult friend of the group (him being of European nationality), I explained it like this:

In the 80s and 90s in the Unite States, we had something called the Presidential Fitness Challenge where we had some "standard" activities and expectations to be considered fit. I did not tell everyone [attending (sic.)] that the "field" part is this test. Many people have negative experiences in elementary school doing these. So please keep this secret.

The two uninitiated nephews were led to believe you cannot be president without meeting our current president's physical capabilities. No one bothered to correct this misinformation as it later became a bit of a running joke.

Somewhere among the group after the great reveal, I heard the word "redemption" here and there. It really was! Many participants surprised themselves with their current capabilities having exceeded their personal benchmarks set half-their-age-or-more ago, although, perhaps with some residual soreness over the next couple of days.

While performance was the general goal of my guests, making sure everyone had fun was mine. Three and a half hours in the morning sun and six hours in the glow of a few cold bubbly drinks later, I would say we all hit our personal targets and could call the weekend well spent!
