Xanga Digest Emails

Posted on: Thu 12 December 2024

I am keeping the old emails from Xanga Digest from a thousand years ago.

It is really interesting to read the thoughts of my peers from our most stressful and vulnerable years. At least, they were the first times we experienced the amount of pain we could self-inflict via obligation.

What I am trying to say is that, as adults, there's a lot of pressure with real world consequences that we didn't even sign up for. The pressure comes from other people (like coworkers), non-human entities (like banks), and societal pressure (like social media.) But in high school, the pressure often does not result in real-world consequences. Let's say you decided not to audition for that concert ensemble? Would you still go to college? Yes. Would you still have friends? Yes. Well, then, exactly what would really change? Nothing. Maybe you'd save some $$ or re-allocate that time to another type of art.

It is really wonderful to read these raw accounts of people I once cared for because it reflects a time when us millenials weren't so afraid. As teens, we wanted to be seen so badly that most of us put some pretty gnarly and personal things openly on the internet. One just had to have your link (or a bad habit of not deleting emails.) As adults, we want so badly to be seen a certain WAY which is such a cruel nuance.

FUTURE LIZ EDIT HERE: I found this entries sitting in my Freewrite and am really interested in where I was going here. I think I had a lot of hesitation about sharing my authentic personality online. I still do.

But lately (it's almost a year since this was originally written,) I've been thinking about how much I appreciate the personal blog.

I recently found blogroll.org and subscribed to some random people's online journals. They all have a disclaimer that their website is unrefined. But honestly, that's what I am looking for - no disclaimer needed!

It's been tough to find a place where people are authentic on the internet. I suppose, like those I started to subscribe to, I can also be a part of the internet I want to see. Democratizing a space isn't about wishing, it's about contributing as well. OKAY FUTURE LIZ EDIT OVER.
